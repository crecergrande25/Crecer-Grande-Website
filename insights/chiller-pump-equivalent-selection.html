How to Select an Equivalent Laser Chiller Pump

A practical guide to matching a replacement laser chiller pump by voltage, flow, head, ports, mounting, fluid duty and control rather than wattage alone.

Send Related Requirement →More Resources
Use this as a technical-preparation guide.

Actual compatibility, process settings and engineering decisions still depend on the equipment, material, drawing and operating condition of the specific requirement.

Electrical rating is only the first filter

Match rated voltage, frequency and motor power, but do not stop there. A pump with the same wattage can produce a very different flow/head curve.

Flow and head define the hydraulic job

Flow tells you how much coolant can move; head describes the pressure capability needed to overcome system resistance. Both have to be appropriate for the actual circuit.

Ports and mounting determine whether it can be installed

Record inlet/outlet size, thread/connection type, orientation, mounting-hole pattern and the physical envelope available inside the chiller.

Check fluid and temperature duty

Material compatibility, coolant type and operating temperature can affect seal and pump suitability.

Verify after installation

A successful replacement should be checked for stable circulation, leakage, noise/vibration, alarms, temperature control and machine operation — not only whether the pump spins.
