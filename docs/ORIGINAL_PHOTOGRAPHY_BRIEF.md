# Original photo and video brief

Release 8 uses a new, explicitly labelled generated illustration set at the owner’s request. This brief is for future replacement with actual company photographs; the old unverified library remains retired.

The legacy illustration library is withdrawn from public layouts. Do not restore it under captions that suggest real completed company work. The site is intentionally text-led until approved photographs are supplied. This is an unfinished requirement, not a claim that text replaces photography.

## Capture one consistent set

Use the same clean bench or workshop background, neutral lighting and white balance. Keep the material's actual color and surface condition. Avoid artificial logos on parts, unrelated branded equipment and customer information. Obtain permission from people shown and customers whose parts or drawings are identifiable.

Provide the original files with the date, photographer, process, material, job context and permission to publish. Original JPEGs or high-quality WebP are suitable; keep originals outside the public source if they contain private EXIF/location or customer information. Do not use AI-generated scenes or invented before/after shots.

| Placement | Photograph required | Suggested crop |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | One real workshop view or finished part, clear area for the short headline | Wide landscape, at least 2000 px wide |
| Four service cards | CAD bench, fabrication process, maintenance work and dimensional inspection | Matching landscape crops |
| Laser Welding | Actual welding setup and the resulting seam on a named material | One wide process image and close seam detail |
| Chiller guide | Own pump/nameplate, ports and installation, permission verified | Show useful details without customer identifiers |
| Reverse engineering | Worn or broken sample, then replacement or approved drawing | Same angle and scale for both |
| Laser marking | Blank component, then finished mark | Same bench, angle and lighting |
| Laser cutting | Material before cutting, then cleaned finished part | Same material and job |

Use `object-fit: contain` for technical evidence where an edge, hole or nameplate must stay visible. For cards, approve the crop on desktop and at 390 px; never stretch the image. Write alt text describing the visible action, object and material, not the company name or an unsupported result.

## Short process video

Provide a 10–15 second original cutting or welding clip, filmed with appropriate equipment and workshop protection. Do not film exposed laser light unsafely for the sake of the clip. Supply MP4/WebM, a real poster frame and a short text description. Use controls and `preload="metadata"`; avoid autoplay and respect reduced-motion preferences. No video is embedded in this release because none was supplied.

Before publishing, replace the solid hero with ONE approved original photo, add the four matching card photos, and record sources/consent in the photography registry. Update the media integrity guard to allow only those approved images; do not reinstate the old enforced image-count policy.
