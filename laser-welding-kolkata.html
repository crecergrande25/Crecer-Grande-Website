What to send

  • Drawing, STEP file, sketch or clear photographs
  • Material grade and thickness
  • Joint type and weld length or location
  • Quantity
  • Finish requirement (seam appearance, cleaning, polishing)
  • Any leak-tightness or strength requirement

Important scope note

Final feasibility, joint design, material compatibility, inspection level, price and lead time are confirmed after review. Pressure-containing, code-governed or safety-critical welds are not assumed and need to be discussed first.
