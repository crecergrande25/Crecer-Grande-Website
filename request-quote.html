What helps us review faster

Technical evidence beats a long description.

  • Part number, make / model or nameplate
  • Photos showing the complete item and connection points
  • Drawing, CAD or sample dimensions
  • Material, quantity, tolerance and finish
  • Machine alarm, recent intervention and symptom
  • NCR or measured values where applicable

What happens next

A requirement is reviewed before it becomes a quotation.

That review helps avoid quoting the wrong part, wrong process or incomplete scope when important information is still missing.

01

Requirement received

Files, reference details and the stated requirement are checked for usable evidence.

02

Engineering review

Missing information, interfaces, compatibility and technical route are identified.

03

Clarification / route

We request any essential missing information and define the relevant service path.

04

Quotation / next action

Once the scope is sufficiently defined, the commercial or technical next step can be issued.
