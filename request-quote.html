What helps us review faster

Technical evidence is more useful than a long description.

  • Part number, make / model or nameplate
  • Photos showing the complete item and connection points
  • Drawing / CAD / sample dimensions
  • Material, quantity, tolerance and finish
  • Machine alarm, recent intervention and symptom
  • NCR, measured values or tender document where applicable
Compatibility-sensitive items: machine, cutting-head, chiller or model confirmation may be required before an equivalent part is recommended.
For native CAD assemblies: IAM, SLDASM and similar files may need their referenced component files. ZIP the complete assembly package or export a STEP assembly where possible.
