Project / capability example

Requirement-led execution

This capability combines suitable metal keychains or blanks with clean permanent marking for names, logos, short text, identifiers or QR references. Final finish and marking contrast depend on material, coating and laser response.

  • Customer artwork / text reviewed before marking
  • Material and surface condition confirmed
  • Sample / first article can be agreed for repeat quantities
  • Batch marking and packing arranged to requirement
