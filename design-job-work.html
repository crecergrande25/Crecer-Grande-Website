Piping work

Mechanical piping layout and drafting support

Piping job work is scoped around the available plant/equipment information and the level of engineering responsibility required.

Typical drafting / layout scope

  • Equipment and nozzle-based line routing
  • General piping layouts and routing modifications
  • Pipe support / bracket concepts and drawings
  • Selected isometric / fabrication-style drawings
  • Line / component schedules and MTO support
  • Existing-layout updates and as-built drafting from supplied site data

Engineering boundary

Where a project requires pressure-design calculations, stress analysis, code certification, statutory approval or professional sign-off, that requirement is identified during review and handled only within the competence/authorization available for the project, including qualified third parties where needed.

This keeps drafting support, layout engineering and regulated pressure-system responsibility clearly separated before the commercial scope is confirmed.
