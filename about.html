What Crecer Grande is

A practical interface between an industrial requirement and a usable next step.

Industrial requirements may begin with a worn component, machine alarm, unclear nameplate, CAD file, NCR, customer drawing or a part that is difficult to identify. The first task is to understand what is actually needed.

From there, the requirement can move toward design, manufacturing, maintenance, automation or inspection without forcing the customer to restart the conversation.

How CG works

Four operating principles.

The aim is to make the work disciplined and useful even when the original requirement is incomplete.

01

Evidence first

Use drawings, dimensions, photos, nameplates, alarms, samples and operating data before assuming a cause or equivalent.

02

Compatibility first

Review model-dependent parts against actual interfaces and specifications rather than matching by appearance.

03

FAI where risk matters

Use first-article thinking where geometry, fit or repeat-manufacture risk makes an early correction valuable.

04

Document the route

Record findings, revisions, verification and follow-up when the scope benefits from traceable evidence.
