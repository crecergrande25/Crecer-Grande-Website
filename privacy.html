Privacy

Website enquiries and technical files.

Information submitted through the CG website is used to review and respond to the stated engineering or commercial requirement.

Technical file handling

Uploaded CAD, drawings, photos and enquiry details may be stored in the connected website backend for technical review and follow-up. Do not submit files you are not authorized to share.

Website analytics

To understand website use and improve navigation, the site may store a persistent anonymous visitor identifier and a browser-session identifier. Analytics records may include the page viewed, page title, referring page, campaign parameters, device category, browser or user-agent information, operating-system/platform information, language and screen size.

The session identifier is limited to the current browser session, while the anonymous visitor identifier may persist on the device. These identifiers are used for website analytics and are not intended to identify a visitor by name.

Infrastructure and requests

The website may use third-party infrastructure required to operate hosting, secure data storage and browser libraries. Public enquiry forms and technical-file uploads are used only to process the submitted engineering or commercial requirement and associated follow-up.

Contact Crecer Grande if you need a submitted enquiry corrected or removed.
