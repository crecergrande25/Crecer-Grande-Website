Frequently asked questions

Before you send the requirement

Can you make a part without a drawing?

Potentially yes, using an available sample, measurement and functional information.

Can you make one piece only?

One-off requirements can be reviewed, although process choice and commercial viability depend on the component.

Can material be matched exactly?

Material selection is based on available specification, evidence, testing if separately scoped, and functional requirements. Exact equivalence should not be assumed without evidence.

Need a custom or obsolete machine spare developed?

Send the available drawing, sample, files, photographs, machine details or problem statement.
