Product scope

Requirement-led supply, not guesswork.

Replacement circulation-pump identification and sourcing for laser cooling systems. Laser power alone does not determine compatibility; flow, head, voltage, ports, dimensions and the exact chiller configuration are reviewed.

Compatibility note: A pump is quoted only after the installed chiller and hydraulic/electrical duty have been reviewed.

What to send us

  • Chiller make and exact model
  • Pump nameplate / part number
  • Voltage and frequency
  • Flow and head, if available
  • Port size / thread / mounting dimensions
  • Photographs and quantity
