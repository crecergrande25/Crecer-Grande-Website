Start with the pump duty — not only the laser wattage.

These pump models serve different coolant-flow and pressure duties. Laser power can be a useful starting reference, but final replacement selection must also match the actual chiller, existing pump model, voltage, ports, mounting and hydraulic requirement.

Compatibility rule: The recommendations below are starting points for enquiry. Crecer Grande verifies the installed chiller and pump details before confirming supply.
