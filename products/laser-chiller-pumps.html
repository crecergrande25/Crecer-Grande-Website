Pump matching

Seven data points before calling a pump “equivalent”.

01

Voltage / frequency

220–240 V / 50 Hz, three-phase or another supply.

02

Motor input

Useful, but never sufficient by itself.

03

Rated flow

The pump has to sustain the required circuit flow.

04

Head / pressure

Needed to overcome restriction through laser, optics and piping.

05

Ports

Thread, bore and inlet/outlet orientation.

06

Mounting / size

Envelope and mounting points have to fit the chiller.

07

Coolant / temperature

Materials and duty must suit the actual cooling circuit.
