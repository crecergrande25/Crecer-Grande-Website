Identify the machine first, then verify the chiller duty.

A pump model alone usually does not tell you whether the system is a fiber-laser cutting machine or a laser-welding machine. Cutting, welding, cleaning and other systems can overlap in pump family. The strongest identification comes from the machine type + chiller nameplate + installed pump label + laser power.

Compatibility rule: This page gives a starting shortlist only. Final replacement selection must match voltage/frequency, pump duty, ports, mounting and the actual chiller configuration.
Fiber laser cutting

Typical clues

  • Sheet bed or tube/profile cutting machine
  • Often larger standalone dual-circuit chiller
  • Commonly 1500W, 2000W, 3000W, 6000W and above
  • Higher flow/head demand is common, but chiller model still decides the pump
Laser welding / cleaning

Typical clues

  • Handheld torch or robotic head
  • Often compact or medium-duty chiller
  • May overlap with smaller cutting-machine pump families
  • Confirm the chiller and pump nameplates before ordering
