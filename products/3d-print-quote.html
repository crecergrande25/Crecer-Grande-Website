Why the quote is not “₹ per gram”

Weight is only one part of 3D-print manufacturing cost.

Real cost changes with machine time, layer height, orientation, supports, post-processing, inspection, inserts, quantity and failure risk.

GEOMETRY

Material volume

Model volume and density are useful, but FDM infill and support strategy change actual material usage.

TIME

Machine time

Layer height, part height, quantity and support complexity can matter more than raw material cost.

SECONDARY

Finishing & inspection

Brass inserts, sanding, painting, FAI and assembly add real engineering and handling time.
