# Crecer Grande Engineering Insights — Editorial Guide

## Purpose
Engineering Insights is a practical technical knowledge hub for engineers, technicians, manufacturing MSMEs, purchase teams and students.

## Article standard
Every article should:
1. Solve one specific engineering question.
2. Start with a short practical answer.
3. Separate rules of thumb from standards/OEM requirements.
4. State assumptions and limits.
5. Use technically relevant images only.
6. Avoid presenting machine-specific values as universal settings.
7. Cite authoritative manufacturer/standards/training references where useful.
8. End with a clear engineering disclaimer and relevant Crecer Grande CTA.

## Preferred topic structure
- Problem / question
- Quick answer
- Engineering explanation
- Formula / table / practical method
- Common mistakes
- When the rule does not apply
- References
- Related CG service/tool

## Safety / accuracy rule
The drawing, current governing standard, OEM manual, tooling supplier data, approved process sheet and qualified engineering judgment always take priority over a website guide.
