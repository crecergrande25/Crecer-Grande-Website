When one requirement crosses divisions

The customer still gets one connected route.

Divisions describe specialist capability; they are not walls between teams. A requirement can move across disciplines when the outcome needs it.

Design → Make → Verify

New or modified component

Engineering defines the part, manufacturing executes the agreed route, and inspection closes critical acceptance points.

Diagnose → Identify → Restore

Machine breakdown or obsolete spare

Maintenance evidence can lead into spare identification, reverse engineering, custom manufacture or automation support.

Standardise → Inspect → Improve

Quality-system or recurring defect issue

QMS, inspection, NCR/RCA/CAPA and records can be coordinated around the same operational problem.
