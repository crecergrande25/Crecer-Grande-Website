Where we can help

PLC and HMI assessment

Review existing PLC/HMI makes, models, panel documentation, I/O lists, faults and requested functional changes. Scope and compatibility depend on equipment access and available information.

Sensors, controls and interfaces

Identification and review of sensors, drives, actuators, signal wiring and control interfaces for existing or proposed equipment.

Troubleshooting and retrofit planning

Structure reported faults, evidence and requirements, then define a practical verification, replacement, modification or integration approach.

What to send for a useful review

  • Machine and controller manufacturer, model, serial or nameplate photographs
  • PLC/HMI model, panel photographs, schematics and current fault messages
  • Brief explanation of what happens now and what you need changed
  • Available I/O lists, electrical drawings, programs or specifications you are authorized to share
  • Machine location, urgency and access constraints

Final deliverables, commissioning obligations, safety measures and scope are determined after technical review. Never bypass machine safety controls or undertake energized work without qualified personnel.
