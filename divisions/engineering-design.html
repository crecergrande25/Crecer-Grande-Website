Engineering that moves the job forward

A CAD file is useful only when the next person can act on it.

Good engineering design reduces ambiguity before production, sourcing, inspection or assembly begins. We structure the available evidence, identify the missing information and convert it into a practical technical output.

The starting point can be a sketch, sample, worn component, PDF drawing, dimension sheet, photograph or an existing model that needs correction.

A controlled engineering route

From evidence to an actionable technical deliverable.

The workflow is intentionally simple: confirm what exists, identify what is missing, develop the engineering output, then check it before release.

Useful starting inputs

You do not need a perfect brief.

  • Existing drawing, PDF, sketch or old CAD
  • Physical sample or component photographs
  • Critical dimensions and mating-part information
  • Material, load, environment or application constraints
  • Manufacturing process or supplier limitations
  • Required output format and revision expectation
Typical deliverables

Clear engineering outputs.

  • 3D part, assembly and layout models
  • 2D production, fabrication and inspection drawings
  • Piping layouts and related design drawings
  • Machine / fixture / component design documentation
  • BOM, MTO and assembly documentation where applicable
  • Reverse-engineered geometry, DFM/DFA observations and controlled revisions
