Working method

Requirement → review → execution → verification

01

Define

Clarify the real functional / commercial requirement and missing information.

02

Review

Check compatibility, feasibility, risk, scope and the evidence needed.

03

Execute

Proceed through the agreed design, supply, manufacturing, service or documentation route.

04

Verify

Confirm output, inspection, record or follow-up appropriate to the scope.

What to send

  • Drawing / specification
  • Acceptance criteria / critical dimensions
  • Sample size
  • Stage of inspection
  • Supplier / location information
  • Required report / photo evidence

Scope discipline

Crecer Grande confirms the actual deliverable, technical assumptions, third-party dependencies and commercial terms before execution. Where authorization, accreditation, statutory sign-off or certification authority is required, that role must be provided by the appropriate authorized / accredited party.
