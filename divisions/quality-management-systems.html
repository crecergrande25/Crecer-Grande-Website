QMS lifecycle

A useful system connects business context to measurable improvement.

Documentation has value only when responsibilities, execution, evidence and improvement remain connected.

01 • Context

Understand the organisation

Scope, processes, interested parties, requirements, risks and responsibilities.

02 • Plan

Define the controls

Set process ownership, criteria, documented information and practical control points.

03 • Operate

Run the process

Use procedures, forms, registers and records that support the real workflow.

04 • Check

Verify performance

Inspect evidence through audits, reviews, NCRs, KPIs and effectiveness checks.

05 • Improve

Close the loop

Use RCA, CAPA, lessons learned and management review to reduce recurrence.

NCR → RCA → CAPA

Corrective action should close a verified mechanism, not a plausible story.

A professional CAPA route protects the customer first, verifies the cause second, and checks effectiveness after implementation.

01

Define

State the nonconformity precisely: what, where, when, quantity, requirement and detection point.

02

Contain

Protect the customer and ongoing production while permanent action is still under investigation.

03

Verify cause

Use evidence to confirm the mechanism instead of accepting the first plausible explanation.

04

Correct

Change the system, process, control or design at the point that created the verified cause.

05

Check effectiveness

Verify that the corrective action reduced recurrence risk and produced the intended result.

Audit readiness

Do not prepare for an audit by hiding how the process really works.

Readiness means the documented system and actual practice agree, records are available, people understand their roles, and open gaps have a credible action path.

The strongest audit preparation is therefore operational: verify process evidence, close obvious inconsistencies and make responsibilities visible.

ProcessIs the workflow defined?

Inputs, outputs, ownership, sequence and criteria should be clear.

EvidenceCan the activity be demonstrated?

Records should support what the organisation says it does.

PeopleDo responsibilities match practice?

Competence, awareness and accountability need to be visible in day-to-day work.

ImprovementAre known gaps being closed?

Open findings need owners, actions, target dates and effectiveness checks.
