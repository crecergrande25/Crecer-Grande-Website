Problem-solving approach

RCA starts with evidence, not a guessed cause.

The Quality route captures a measurable problem statement, detection point, extent, containment and available evidence. It can then be developed into 5 Why, fishbone, CAPA or a formal 8D workflow as needed.

1. Define

What happened, where, when, to which product or process, and how much is affected?

2. Contain

Protect the customer and production first. Segregation, replacement, 100% checks or machine hold are recorded separately from root cause.

3. Verify cause & action

Potential causes must be supported by evidence. Corrective action is not complete until effectiveness is verified.

How CG applies engineering skills

Typical problem patterns the desk is designed for.

Examples are intentionally generalized and anonymized. Customer names, proprietary drawings and confidential commercial information are not published here.

Machine / RCA

Laser cutting quality suddenly deteriorates

Structure the check around optics, cleanliness, nozzle condition/centering, focus, assist gas, material and recent maintenance rather than changing random parameters.

Sourcing

Chiller pump fails and an equivalent is needed

Match electrical rating, hydraulic duty, dimensions, ports, mounting, fluid compatibility and operating environment before proposing a replacement.

Design / Site

A replacement component does not fit on site

Compare drawing dimensions, field measurements, measurement method and tolerance stack before modifying the part or machine.

3D printing

FAI reveals print-related dimensional shift

Use first-article feedback to revise geometry, print orientation or process settings before committing the full batch.

Quality / CAPA

Customer NCR traces back to the wrong production instruction

Contain the suspect output, verify the actual root cause, correct the data/BOM/workflow and add a control that prevents recurrence.

QMS

Manufacturer needs ISO 9001 audit readiness

Build the system around the actual process flow: document control, purchasing, production, inspection, NCR/CAPA, calibration, audit and management review records.

Engineering recommendations remain subject to actual drawings, machine manuals, site conditions, measurements, applicable standards, manufacturer instructions and formal quotation/scope confirmation.
