Problem-solving approach

Evidence first. Cause second. Action third.

RCA should distinguish the symptom, containment, verified cause, corrective action and effectiveness check.

1. DefineWhat happened, where, when, how much, to which product/process and how was it detected?
2. ContainProtect the customer and production before permanent corrective action is claimed.
3. Verify causePotential causes need evidence. A plausible story is not the same as a verified mechanism.
4. Verify actionCorrective action is complete only after recurrence risk and effectiveness are reviewed.
Typical problem patterns

Designed for industrial evidence, not generic contact messages.

Machine / RCA

Laser cutting quality deteriorates

Review optics, cleanliness, nozzle, centering, focus, gas, material and recent interventions systematically.

Sourcing

Chiller pump fails

Match voltage, hydraulic duty, ports, mounting, fluid and operating environment before selecting an alternative.

Quality

Customer NCR needs CAPA

Contain suspect output, verify root cause, correct the system and verify effectiveness.
