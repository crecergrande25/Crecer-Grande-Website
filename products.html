Compatibility first

Why industrial sourcing cannot rely on a photograph alone.

Mechanical fit is only one layer. Electrical, hydraulic, optical, control and duty parameters often decide whether a part is actually suitable.

01

Verify interfaces

Dimensions, mounting, thread, connector and physical fit may be critical.

02

Check operating data

Voltage, current, pressure, flow, power, wavelength or duty conditions can change the answer.

03

Use machine context

Machine, head, controller, chiller or system model helps eliminate false equivalents.

04

Select the right route

OEM reference, compatible alternative, custom manufacture and reverse engineering are different solutions.
