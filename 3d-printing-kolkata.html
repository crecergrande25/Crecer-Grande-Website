Process selection

Different print processes solve different problems.

Choose by function, surface, material properties, geometry, quantity and inspection requirement.

ESTIMATOR

FDM / FFF

Economical functional prototypes, jigs, fixtures and engineering polymer parts.

  • PLA / PETG / ABS / ASA
  • TPU / Nylon / PA-CF / PC
  • Infill, layer height and colour options
ESTIMATOR

SLA / DLP Resin

Fine-detail and smooth-surface prototypes and small components.

  • Standard / tough / clear
  • Flexible / high-temp via RFQ where applicable
  • Post-cure and finishing review
RFQ

SLS Nylon

Complex nylon parts and low-volume production without conventional support structures.

  • PA12 / PA11
  • Glass-filled options where available
  • Nesting economics reviewed by project
RFQ

MJF

Production-oriented functional nylon and flexible parts.

  • PA12 and selected TPU routes
  • Batch / finish / dye review
  • Availability confirmed per project
SPECIALIST RFQ

  • Presentation prototypes
  • Colour / texture requirements
  • Project-specific availability
